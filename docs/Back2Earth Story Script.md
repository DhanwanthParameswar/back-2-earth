Game Description:

2D space game about an unfortunate collision with a black hole. Turning it into an from the current spaceship to a space pod to get back to Earth 16x16 pixel scale

Story Plot: 

**1\. Almost home**

A small pixel-art spaceship crosses a dark starfield. Earth glows in the distance, using the same blue and teal colors as the main menu.ded

The stars move slowly across separate background layers. A tiny engine flame flickers.

Fade into the crew cabin.

Three astronauts rest strapped into their seats. Their different suit colors make each character immediately recognizable. A small monitor displays a blinking Earth icon:

**DESTINATION: EARTH**

Quiet engine hum. Soft electronic beeps.

One astronaut wakes. A small dialogue bubble appears:

**“Almost home.”**

**2\. The first impact**

A sudden metallic crash.

The screen shakes sharply. One brief impact flash lights the cabin. The astronauts jerk against their restraints, and a loose cup floats across the room.

The ceiling lights go dark.

A short pause.

Red emergency lights activate in alternating sections of the corridor.

**SHIP SYSTEM:**  
**“IMPACT DETECTED. EVACUATE.”**

The astronauts wake and close their helmet visors with small clicks.

The crew leader points toward the exit.

**“Pods. Move\!”**

**3\. The breach**

Green floor arrows blink toward the escape pods.

The three astronauts move through the corridor. The camera follows them using the same perspective as gameplay.

Through a window, Earth slowly shifts out of view as the damaged ship begins to turn.

A second crash.

Wall tiles break outward. A short burst of air carries loose objects and fragments through the opening.

The astronaut nearest the breach loses their grip on a handrail.

The player lunges toward them.

For one beat, their outstretched hands almost meet.

Then the astronaut passes beyond reach.

Cut to a wide exterior shot. The small, brightly colored sprite drifts away from the ship.

All sound drops out.

Their helmet light blinks once.

**4\. Seal the door**

Back inside, the remaining crewmate pulls the player toward the emergency bulkhead.

A broken piece of metal blocks the door.

The crewmate moves it aside while the player reaches the wall control.

Above the control, a red pixel icon flashes:

**SEAL SECTION**

The player presses it.

The heavy door slides shut, hiding the damaged corridor.

A deep mechanical thud.

The rushing air stops.

**5\. Two remain**

Hold the camera still.

The two astronauts stand beside the sealed door. The player remains turned toward it.

Only helmet breathing and a faint alarm can be heard.

**PLAYER:** “Come in.”

A radio crackle.

No response.

A nearby terminal flickers:

**PRESSURE: STABLE**  
**POWER: OFFLINE**  
**ESCAPE PODS: DISCONNECTED**

The remaining crewmate moves beside the player.

**“Get the power back. Then we find the escape pods.”**

The player turns toward the dark corridor ahead.

Fade to black.

Your pixel-art **Back2Earth** logo appears.

**6\. Gameplay begins**

Fade back into the same room, with both characters in the same positions.

The emergency terminal blinks amber.

**OBJECTIVE: RESTORE EMERGENCY POWER**

The player gains control.
